Mahjong gameplay previews — captured October 4, 2026

mahjong-peaks-v1.27.0.mp4, mahjong-butterfly-v1.27.0.mp4 and
mahjong-fortress-v1.27.0.mp4 show matching pairs in official Blanc v1.27.0
for macOS. Peaks and Fortress use Burst Auto; Butterfly uses Classic.
Native window recordings are trimmed at normal speed and exported at 30fps.
No game UI or motion was generated. The app chrome is cropped out and game
content is scaled proportionally. Clips are silent and load only on opening
the Mahjong preview.
Companion WebP files are first-frame posters for paused/reduced-motion previews.
Source and asset hashes: docs/website-revamp-assets.json in the repository.

Mouse gesture demo: illustrative cursor, trail and page transitions over the existing native NASA Roman Island capture (Blanc v1.27.0) and Billboard dawn capture (Blanc v1.25.0). The page transitions and perspective are website presentation, not a recording of the native gesture overlay. Existing NASA and wallpaper credits above apply.
