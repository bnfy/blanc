## Distribution rule

For every proposed batch:

1. For every feature-led post, state the user's tension and experiential
   payoff before explaining the shipped mechanism. If the reader still has to
   ask “why should I care?”, the copy is not ready. Feature-state terms such
   as “quiet” or “awake” do not count as a payoff by themselves.
2. Check the destination platform and campaign against this ledger.
3. Recheck the live thread immediately before every reply to prevent a second
   Blanc response.
4. Separate conversation-first outreach from product promotion; most replies
   should not pitch Blanc.
5. Give every priority platform an intentional next action or an evidence-based
   reason to hold. Do not let one platform absorb the batch by default.
6. Record follower/subscriber counts and live URLs in the canonical analytics
   log after publication.
7. Follow `docs/marketing-claims.md` and `docs/brand-usage.md` for every new
   asset.

Reuse of a retired asset or hook requires a specific rationale and explicit
user approval. Silence is not approval.
