Public Blanc baseline: **v1.10.0**.

## Publication copy

privacy features live inside business models.

before trusting a browser's privacy settings, ask what the browser needs from
you to make money.

Blanc is built by a small independent studio. no venture funding, no investors
setting the product agenda, and no ads business. the browser is free; Patron
support is optional.

that does not mean “nothing leaves your computer.” on a fresh install, the
limited usage-ping choice starts on. Blanc asks before the first ping, and you
can turn it off before continuing.

follow if you want the details, not just the slogan.
