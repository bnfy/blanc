# Blanc Ambassador pilot launch copy — September 2, 2026

Status: staged only. The page is live. Every publication, pin, feature, like,
reply, or follow remains separately approval-gated.

Voice and formatting follow `voice-and-formatting-canon.md`: normal sentence
capitalization is the default for this professional creator invitation.
Lowercase is not a blanket Blanc house style.

Direct invitations to the five qualified creators are the primary acquisition
action. This public call is a supporting owned-content unit: it gives other
relevant creators a way to discover the program and gives non-creators a reason
to follow the process.
