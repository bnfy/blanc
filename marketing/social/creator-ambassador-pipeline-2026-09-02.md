## Qualification gates

A creator advances only when all are true:

- recent activity and visible audience interaction;
- clear browser, productivity, privacy, design, open-source, or indie-tech fit;
- willingness to form an independent opinion;
- no demand for scripted praise;
- a plausible native format for their audience;
- a trackable destination or measurable response.

## Funnel

| Stage | Success signal | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation | Reply or request for the brief | Send the current-release brief and tracked link after approval |
| Trial | Creator confirms install or shares substantive feedback | Resolve confusion; identify one authentic audience angle |
| First collaboration | Relevant comments, profile visits, follows, site activity, downloads, or useful product feedback | Compare against Blanc's owned baseline |
| 30-day ambassador pilot | Consistent audience fit plus measurable conversion | Offer only with separately approved terms and disclosure plan |
| Renewal | Qualified outcome exceeds cost and workload | Continue, revise, or stop explicitly |

## Ambassador pilot principles

- one creator at a time until the measurement path is reliable;
- creator controls the opinion and wording;
- no exclusivity in the first pilot;
- one native deliverable plus permission to repost only if separately agreed;
- unique UTM link per creator and platform;
- flat-fee or commission terms require separate approval and implementation;
- disclose any compensation or material benefit in the platform-native way;
- no public claim that a creator is an ambassador before both sides agree.

## Measurement

Track invitation response, accepted trial, published collaboration, qualified
profile visits, follows, site sessions, downloads, feedback quality, cost, and
time spent. An accepted message is not follower growth; a large view count
without profile or follow activity is not a win.

The structured operating record is
[`ambassador-pilot-tracker-2026-09-02.json`](ambassador-pilot-tracker-2026-09-02.json).
It captures the pre-outreach audience baseline and separates invitation,

Initial funnel targets are a 40% invitation reply rate, at least one accepted

not substitutes for the standing verified follower-growth target.

## Approval boundary

Scouting and drafting are read-only. Every DM, comment, like, follow,
subscription, collaboration invitation, rate negotiation, paid term, gifted
benefit, repost permission, and public ambassador label requires action-time
approval.

short application form. The form sends a creator's name, email, profile link,
