## The reset

Follower growth is the primary outcome. Replies, likes, follows, subscriptions,
and comments are distribution tools, not the score.

The operating target is **+10 net followers daily on each of X, Threads,
Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, plus +10 external Substack subscribers**:

- **weekly target:** +70 on each channel separately;
- **measurement:** comparable daily observations at 5 p.m. Eastern; record
  met, missed or not measurable, with actual observation time and cache delay;
- **revision trigger:** three consecutive measured misses on a channel prompt
  one focused experiment for review; missing observations are not misses;
- **quality check:** growth must come with relevant profile visits, meaningful
  engagement, site activity, or downloads—not indiscriminate follow-backs.

These are planning thresholds, not predictions or public claims. Counts are
recorded daily, but experiments are judged after 24 hours and seven days.
The former +35 portfolio minimum and lower platform floors are superseded;
growth on one channel does not offset another channel's shortfall. Exclude the
Substack author account and keep attributed follows separate from net growth.

## Why the previous loop changes

On September 2, two approved outreach waves produced 28 public actions without
an immediate verified follower gain on X or Threads. By contrast, the new
Facebook ad-blocking post produced one verified follow from 79 viewers. Its
active Page Visits promotion had reached 77 people and generated 32 Facebook
Page visits after $4.81 of spend—about $0.15 per visit.

This is early evidence, not a final ad verdict, but it makes the priority clear:
build and distribute posts that convert non-followers, then use replies to put
those posts and the profile in front of aligned people.

## The daily driver loop

1. **Baseline:** record follower/subscriber totals, new inbound, yesterday's
   non-follower reach, profile visits, follow attribution, site activity, and
   downloads where available.
2. **Choose one growth bet:** one platform-native owned post, one creator
   collaboration pitch, or one paid-creative decision. Never start a new asset
   merely because the previous task is slow.
3. **Build the conversion path:** verify the profile promise, pinned/featured
   post, destination, and tracking before distribution.
4. **Distribute selectively:** place the owned idea into two to five current,
   relevant conversations or creator relationships. A reply must either reach
   the intended audience, answer real inbound, or advance a relationship.
5. **Measure at 60–90 minutes and 24 hours:** record non-follower reach,
   profile visits, follows, watch retention, meaningful responses, shares,
   saves, site activity, and downloads.
6. **End-of-day decision:** scale, revise, or stop. Log the reason and carry
   only the highest-value unfinished item into the next day.

Useful routine inbound replies are authorized under the September 4 plan.
Read the full conversation and shared log, verify product claims, and verify
the submitted reply before recording success. Escalate sensitive issues.
New posts, creator outreach, pins, profile changes and ad changes remain in
the separate review queue. Scheduled ad reviews are recommendation-only.
This authorization does not expand into unsolicited engagement actions.

### Work-in-progress limit

Keep one ordered action queue and finish its highest-priority unfinished item
before starting another campaign. At any time, allow at most:

- one time-sensitive public action awaiting approval;
- one creator-outreach batch awaiting approval; and
- one read-only measurement or response-check task in progress.

A genuinely expiring opportunity may move to the front, but it does not erase
the displaced item; record both the reason and the next resume point. Do not
start a new asset because an approval is pending. Use that time for claim
verification, creator qualification, measurement, or the scheduled response
check already in the queue. At the start of every continuation, resume the
first incomplete item rather than rebuilding the plan from scratch.

## Content system

Use a rolling four-part mix so Blanc does not become a feature inventory or a
tab-joke account:

- **40% product proof:** a real current-release interaction with a plain human
  payoff;
- **25% founder/build decisions:** what Blanc chose, what tradeoff it accepted,
  and why;
- **20% browser tension and industry point of view:** privacy, business models,
  interface bloat, and user agency;
- **15% audience participation:** questions, challenges, naming prompts, and
  requests for honest reactions.

Every owned post follows the release-backed gate in
`docs/marketing-claims.md`:

1. familiar tension;
2. emotional or practical payoff;
3. shipped mechanism;
4. material qualification;
5. a specific reason to follow Blanc.

The follow reason should promise a useful continuing story—for example, seeing
the decisions behind a different desktop browser—not merely say “follow us.”

## Creator acquisition and ambassador pipeline

Each weekday, scout five small-to-mid-sized creators in browser, productivity,
privacy, design, open-source, Linux, Windows, macOS, or indie-tech communities.
A candidate must have posted within 24 hours, visibly interact with their
audience, and have an audience that plausibly cares about Blanc.

For the best candidates:

- prepare a short personalized invitation to try the current public build;
- ask for an honest reaction, not scripted praise;
- connect the invitation to something the creator already discusses;
- provide a platform-specific tracked link;
- prefer native collaborations, reaction videos, interviews, or build
  conversations over generic comment drops.

Treat “ambassador” as an earned second stage, not the opening ask:

1. **Product trial:** invite the creator to try the current public build with
   no posting obligation.
2. **Honest reaction:** learn what they understood, liked, or found confusing.
3. **Native collaboration:** test one format that fits their audience—a short
   reaction, founder conversation, workflow comparison, or launch-week post.
4. **Ambassador pilot:** only after the first collaboration shows qualified
   engagement, offer a 30-day relationship with a unique tracked link, a clear
   deliverable, creator control over the opinion, and any required disclosure.
5. **Retain or stop:** continue only when the creator drives relevant profile
   visits, follows, site activity, downloads, or useful product feedback.

Target **two accepted micro-collaborations per week** and **one qualified
ambassador pilot by launch week**. A rate-card request is discovery, not
authorization to spend. No compensation, commission, free Patron benefit,
deliverable, exclusivity, or recurring relationship is offered without a
separate approval. Sending remains approval-gated.

An Instagram collaborator can cover both Instagram and Facebook through
native Meta placement when appropriate. Do not force a separate Facebook-only
creator unless their audience and recent activity are independently strong.

The website supports this funnel at `/ambassadors`. Direct invitations should
use a creator-specific UTM link to that page after it is deployed. The page is
an explanation and introduction path, not an automated acceptance or payment
system; terms still require a direct conversation and separate approval.

## Decision rules

- **Reach without profile visits:** the hook attracted attention but did not
  make Blanc interesting; change the promise or creative.
- **Profile visits without follows:** the profile or pinned/featured content
  did not provide a compelling continuing reason to follow.
- **Follows without site activity:** classify the unit as audience-building;
  do not claim it drove product adoption.
- **Site visits without downloads:** inspect the landing and installer path
  before spending more on distribution.
- **Three reply waves with views but no profile or follower movement:** stop
  that reply pattern. Replies remain only for inbound, high-fit distribution,
  or relationship development.
- **Cross-posted creative underperforms native creative twice:** stop the
  cross-platform adaptation and create for the destination platform first.
- **Follower decline:** pause low-value posting on that platform and test one
  new native hook against non-followers.
- **A winner:** reuse the underlying tension only with new footage, a new hook,
  or a new audience angle; never recycle the same asset.
