## Creator acquisition and ambassador pipeline

Each weekday, scout five small-to-mid-sized creators in browser, productivity,
privacy, design, open-source, Linux, Windows, macOS, or indie-tech communities.
A candidate must have posted within 24 hours, visibly interact with their
audience, and have an audience that plausibly cares about Blanc.

For the best candidates:

- prepare a short personalized invitation to try the current public build;
- ask for an honest reaction, not scripted praise;
- connect the invitation to something the creator already discusses;
- provide a platform-specific tracked link;
- prefer native collaborations, reaction videos, interviews, or build
  conversations over generic comment drops.

Treat “ambassador” as an earned second stage, not the opening ask:

1. **Product trial:** invite the creator to try the current public build with
   no posting obligation.
2. **Honest reaction:** learn what they understood, liked, or found confusing.
3. **Native collaboration:** test one format that fits their audience—a short
   reaction, founder conversation, workflow comparison, or launch-week post.
4. **Ambassador pilot:** only after the first collaboration shows qualified
   engagement, offer a 30-day relationship with a unique tracked link, a clear
   deliverable, creator control over the opinion, and any required disclosure.
5. **Retain or stop:** continue only when the creator drives relevant profile
   visits, follows, site activity, downloads, or useful product feedback.

Target **two accepted micro-collaborations per week** and **one qualified
ambassador pilot by launch week**. A rate-card request is discovery, not
authorization to spend. No compensation, commission, free Patron benefit,
deliverable, exclusivity, or recurring relationship is offered without a
separate approval. Sending remains approval-gated.

An Instagram collaborator can cover both Instagram and Facebook through
native Meta placement when appropriate. Do not force a separate Facebook-only

## Decision rules

- **Reach without profile visits:** the hook attracted attention but did not
  make Blanc interesting; change the promise or creative.
- **Profile visits without follows:** the profile or pinned/featured content
  did not provide a compelling continuing reason to follow.
- **Follows without site activity:** classify the unit as audience-building;
  do not claim it drove product adoption.
- **Site visits without downloads:** inspect the landing and installer path
  before spending more on distribution.
- **Three reply waves with views but no profile or follower movement:** stop
  that reply pattern. Replies remain only for inbound, high-fit distribution,
  or relationship development.
- **Cross-posted creative underperforms native creative twice:** stop the
  cross-platform adaptation and create for the destination platform first.
- **Follower decline:** pause low-value posting on that platform and test one
  new native hook against non-followers.
- **A winner:** reuse the underlying tension only with new footage, a new hook,
  or a new audience angle; never recycle the same asset.
