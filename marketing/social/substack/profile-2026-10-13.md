# Substack profile and publication refresh

Status: **draft for owner review.** Nothing below has been entered into
Substack.

Why: the profile and publication are named "Blanc Browser" and describe only
the browser, but Blanc now also makes Blanc Mail. The bio's "one floating
Island" also reads as if the Island floats over the page, which
[`docs/marketing-claims.md`](../../../docs/marketing-claims.md) rules out; this
draft says "compact" instead. The About page is Substack's default text.

**Unchanged on purpose:** the handle `@blancbrowser` and the address
`blancbrowser.substack.com`. The website footer and earlier posts link to
them, and the blanc.day move keeps social handles and Substack as they are.
Substack can later serve the publication from a blanc.day address (a paid
custom domain) if the owner wants one without "browser" in it.

## About page

> # About Blanc
>
> **Less is more.** Blanc makes calm, independent software: apps that do their
> job and then get out of your way.
>
> **Blanc Browser** is a free, open-source desktop browser for Mac, Windows and
> Linux. One compact Island replaces the usual tab strip and toolbar, and ad
> and tracker blocking is on from the first launch.
> [Download Blanc Browser](https://blanc.day/?ref=substack-about)
>
> **Blanc Mail** is a native Gmail app for Mac, coming soon.
> [Join the waitlist](https://blanc.day/mail?ref=substack-about)
>
> ## What you'll find here
>
> - **The story behind each release.** What changed, why we changed it, and
>   what it doesn't do yet, a few days after each release.
> - **Essays, about every two weeks.** Who your software works for: how apps
>   make money, what they send home, and why privacy lives in the details.
> - **How we build.** Design decisions and honest tradeoffs, including the ones
>   that cost us something.
> - **Notes** a few times a week, with tips, questions and short takes.
>
> ## Who makes Blanc
>
> Blanc is made by Bananify, an independent software studio, with no venture
> funding, no investors and no advertising business. Blanc Browser is free;
> optional Blanc Patron membership supports the work. Blanc Browser's own code
> is MIT-licensed and public on GitHub.
>
