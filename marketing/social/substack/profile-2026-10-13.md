# Substack profile and publication refresh

Status: **live (October 13, 2026).** On the owner's go-ahead, everything
below was entered in Substack: profile name, bio and website link (now
`https://blanc.day`, was `https://blancbrowser.com`); publication name, short
description, Introduction, email sender name, copyright holder, About page
and welcome email. The welcome email leaves out "A person reads every reply",
which was never confirmed, and replaces Substack's starter text (including its
New Reader Survey button). Substack's settings and API returned every new
value after a reload; the signed-out profile, About page and archive show
them.

**Homepage layout:** Substack hides the publication name, short description,
logo and About page under its "Profile" homepage layout. The owner chose to
switch to the "Custom" layout, so `blancbrowser.substack.com` now opens on a
publication homepage with Home, Notes, Archive and About. The publication
logo is still empty under that layout.

Why: the profile and publication are named "Blanc Browser" and describe only
the browser, but Blanc now also makes Blanc Mail. The bio's "one floating
Island" also reads as if the Island floats over the page, which
[`docs/marketing-claims.md`](../../../docs/marketing-claims.md) rules out; this
draft says "compact" instead. The About page is Substack's default text.

**Unchanged on purpose:** the handle `@blancbrowser` and the address
`blancbrowser.substack.com`. The website footer and earlier posts link to
them, and the blanc.day move keeps social handles and Substack as they are.
Substack can later serve the publication from a blanc.day address (a paid
custom domain) if the owner wants one without "browser" in it.
