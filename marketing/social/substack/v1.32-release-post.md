## Post

**Title:** Leaving Arc? Bring your Spaces with you.

**Subtitle:** Blanc Browser 1.32 imports your Arc Spaces, and now speaks
German, Spanish, French and Portuguese.

---

In May 2025, The Browser Company wrote to Arc members that it was no longer
developing Arc the way it used to, while it kept shipping Chromium upgrades
and security fixes
([their letter](https://browsercompany.substack.com/p/letter-to-arc-members-2025)).
Arc still works. But if you've been wondering what to use next, the hard part
of moving usually isn't the new browser. It's the Spaces: the sets of tabs and
folders you built up over months.

Blanc Browser 1.32 is out, and it can bring those with you.

## Import your Arc Spaces with Bring Your Tabs

Bring Your Tabs is Blanc's tool for moving tabs in from another browser, and
it now has an Arc option. Type `/bring-tabs` in the ⌘L panel (Ctrl+L on
Windows and Linux), choose Arc, and pick the Spaces you want.

Each Space comes across with:

- its pinned tabs,
- its Today tabs,
- and its folders, which become tab groups in Blanc.

If you're a Blanc Patron, each Arc Space becomes a space in Blanc Spaces,
Blanc's saved sets of tabs that you can switch between. Without Patron, each
Space opens in its own window. Either way, a tab loads only when you open it,
so a large import doesn't start loading every page at once.

You can also add Arc's top-row Favorites to a Favorites folder named **Arc**.

## What stays in Arc

Blanc reads Arc's sidebar file on your computer and never changes it. It
doesn't connect to your Arc account or keep anything in sync. Your sign-ins,
passwords, history, archived tabs, Notes and Easels stay in Arc, so keep Arc
around until you've signed back in to the sites you need.

Arc never came to Linux. To move to Blanc on Linux, copy the
`StorableSidebar.json` file from your Mac or PC and pick it with
**Choose file…**.

## Blanc now speaks five languages

Blanc's own menus, buttons and settings now come in German, Spanish, French
and Portuguese (Brazil), as well as English. The translations are made by
machine, so if something reads oddly, write to us at
[support@blanc.day](mailto:support@blanc.day).

If your computer is set to one of these languages, Blanc follows it. To pick
a different one, open Settings → General → Language; the change applies the
next time you open Blanc. Blanc translates its own interface, not the web
pages you visit.

## Also in 1.32

- **Named Workspaces is now Blanc Spaces.** Type `/space` in the ⌘L panel to
  switch to a space, or to save the current window as one. Creating spaces is
  part of Blanc Patron.
- **Blanc's website is now blanc.day.** Older links still work.
- **Opening a link from another app now goes straight to the Blanc you have
  open.** Before, Blanc could start a second copy in the background, which
  then failed.

## Get it

Blanc Browser is free for Mac, Windows and Linux. If you already use it,
Blanc will offer you the update. If not,
[download it at blanc.day](https://blanc.day/?ref=substack-v132). Every
change is in the
[changelog](https://blanc.day/changelog?ref=substack-v132).

Blanc isn't a copy of Arc: it doesn't install extensions from the Chrome Web
Store or include an AI assistant. If you rely on either, it may not be the
right fit today.
