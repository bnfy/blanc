## Why this batch

The logged-in dashboards show different bottlenecks by platform:

- X standalone distribution is near zero, so use a high-reach conversation;
- Threads already has reach, so earn profile curiosity with one casual reply;
- Instagram and Facebook have non-follower exposure, so participate in a
  current browser conversation without forcing a Blanc pitch;
- TikTok has active For You distribution and good profile-to-follow movement,
  but needs a genuinely new, native creative with comment/share potential;
- Substack needs network discovery through real conversation, not another
  publication link dropped into Notes.

This deliberately gives all six priority platforms one intentional action and
does not reuse a published asset.

## Action-time safety gate

Before each reply/comment:

1. Open the exact parent post in the logged-in Brave session.
2. Confirm the parent is still live, relevant, and recent enough to justify a
   response.
3. Expand the full thread and confirm Blanc has not already replied.
4. Confirm the displayed account is `@blancbrowser` / Blanc Browser.
5. Stop if the conversation has changed in a way that makes the drafted reply
   misleading, repetitive, opportunistic, or insensitive.

Before the TikTok upload:

1. Preview the complete 16.5-second export, including the cover and end frame.
2. Confirm the mark is black on white or white on black with no accent-colored
   backing composition.
3. Confirm the asset is not already present on the profile.
4. Capture the pre-publication follower count and latest-video baseline.
