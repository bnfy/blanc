# Blanc creator review brief — public v1.12.0

Status: prepared for creators who explicitly accept an invitation. Do not send
this brief, a download link, or a follow-up message without action-time
approval.

## The short version

Blanc is an open-source desktop browser built around one visible decision: the
page should stay in front. Instead of a permanent tab strip and conventional
toolbar, Blanc keeps tabs and controls inside one floating Island.

Public build: **v1.12.0**\
Platforms: **macOS, Windows, and Linux**\
Website: <https://blancbrowser.com>

## What an honest first look should test

1. Open the pages you normally keep around during real work.
2. Use the resting Island, expand it, and switch between those pages.
3. Try the built-in ad and tracker blocking on an ordinary content site; turn
   it off for that site and observe the reload behavior.
4. Press Command-L on macOS or Control-L on Windows/Linux and find an open tab
   by typing part of its title or address.
5. Say what feels clearer, what feels unfamiliar, and where Blanc still gets
   in the way.

There are no required talking points, scripted praise, or positive-review
expectations. Confusion and criticism are useful product feedback. A creator
is not asked to claim that Blanc is faster, safer, more private, lighter, or
better than another browser unless they independently test a specific claim
and describe their method.

## Release-backed boundaries

- Blanc ships no AI assistant or agent browser.
- The Island replaces the permanent horizontal tab strip and conventional
  toolbar; it does not understand tasks or organize tabs automatically.
- Built-in blocking runs in Blanc's network layer with bundled, pinned filter
  lists. It is not an extension and does not promise to block every ad or
  tracker.
- Named Groups and Named Workspaces are user-directed. Blanc does not infer
  group names or task boundaries.
- Quiet Tabs may free renderer memory for eligible inactive background tabs;
  returning to one can reload the page. It does not promise exact resumption
  or broader CPU, battery, heat, or speed benefits.
- Normal browsing does not require an account. Optional features can have
  their own requirements.
- Blanc is open source under MIT for Bananify Creative-owned code and media,
  with reserved trademarks/identity assets and third-party-license carve-outs.
