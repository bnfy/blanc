  everywhere.

## Measurement and stop rules

Record follower/subscriber totals and available reach/profile metrics:

1. immediately before each publication;
2. after 60–90 minutes;
3. after 24 hours;
4. after 72 hours.

Track qualified applications and creator tags separately from generic likes.
Reach without profile activity means the hook needs revision. Profile visits
without follows or applications means the profile/program promise needs
revision. Do not pin, Feature, boost, or repeat the unit until the first
checkpoint provides evidence.
