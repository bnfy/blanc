# Sunrise revision 3 — distinct compositions

The owner rejected revision 2 because every composition repeated the same centered mark, headline and subtitle. Revision 3 keeps Sunrise but changes the visual language by idea: tactile editorial still life and checklists for the trial; warm-ink sculpture and explanatory diagrams for simplicity; oversized typography and conversation prompts for the switching question. Carousel panels progress through different layouts. Titles may be asymmetrical when the composition calls for it.

The original Sunrise mark is composited separately without redrawing or tinting it. AI-created illustrations are conceptual editorial artwork, not product footage or evidence of a shipped feature. No generated person, customer quote or interface screenshot appears.
