Search

Search engine Used when the address bar input isn't a URL
Custom search address Paste the search address with %s where your search words go, for example https://searx.example.org/search?q=%s. It syncs with your other settings. That address isn't valid — your previous search engine is still in use.
Search suggestions Send eligible typed prefixes to your selected search provider, including Google if selected. Private tabs and recognized sensitive-looking input are excluded. Submitting a search sends the completed query even when suggestions are off.
Bangs Type ! followed by a site's code to search that site directly, for example !w for Wikipedia or !yt for YouTube. Type ! alone to see them. It syncs with your other settings.
Your bangs Syncs with your other settings; the device that saves last wins.
