Feel it: open, switch, edit, pick any colour

This prototype is interactive. Click the Work mark, switch spaces, then hover a row and click ••• to edit it. In the editor, the rainbow swatch opens the custom picker, which takes any hex. Try a pale yellow like #F5E663 to see the readability guard work, and watch the dark-mode pill below it. Tick Reduce Motion to compare the motion fallback.
