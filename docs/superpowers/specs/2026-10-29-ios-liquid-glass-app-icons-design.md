# iPhone Liquid Glass app icons — design

Date: 2026-10-29. Status: approved 2026-10-29; standard icon changed to Sunrise Gold the same day. iPhone only.

## Intent

Give Blanc for iPhone real Liquid Glass app icons and let people choose among them. **Sunrise Gold** becomes the standard icon: the Sunrise in cream glass on a gold tile, layered in three glass pieces with a glow under the water lines. A new iPhone-only icon, **Sunrise Dawn**, puts the Sunrise in white glass over a dawn sky, with the sun's reflection glowing through the glass water lines. In Settings → Appearance → App icon, people can switch to Sunrise Dawn, Sunrise, Sunrise Dark, Sunrise Spectrum or Sunrise Amber. Each one is an Icon Composer `.icon`, so iOS 26 and later draw it with the system's glass and its Light, Dark, Clear and Tinted styles.

## What the owner decided

- **Layered and reflective in standard Light.** The owner asked for "a more layered/reflective icon in regular light standard mode than the current sunrise on cream bg" (2026-10-29).
- **Three directions, all kept.** Three directions were rendered with Icon Composer's `ictool` and shown on a real iOS 27.1 Home Screen in an iPhone Air simulator (`assets/2026-10-29-ios-liquid-glass-app-icons/first-options.png`):
  - **A, Dawn:** the Sunrise in white glass over a sky, with an orange glass sun whose reflection shows in the water lines.
  - **B, Gold:** the Sunrise in cream glass on a gold tile, in three glass pieces, with a glow under the water lines.
  - **C, Amber:** the Mac's Sunrise Amber.

  The owner asked to "offer all these options for users, including the two Arc-themed Sunrise icons from our Mac build" (Sunrise Spectrum and Sunrise Amber).
- **Sunrise Gold is the standard icon** for new installs and for anyone who hasn't chosen one. The owner first chose Dawn, then, after the build, said "let's actually make Sunrise Gold icon the default" (2026-10-29).
- **Dawn is iPhone only for now.** The Mac's App icon choices don't change (owner's choice, recommended).

## Goals

- Sunrise Gold is the iPhone's standard icon in every system style.
- Settings → Appearance → App icon offers six icons: Sunrise Gold, Sunrise Dawn, Sunrise, Sunrise Dark, Sunrise Spectrum and Sunrise Amber.
- Every icon file is generated by `npm run brand:build` and checked by `npm run brand:check`, so `assets/sunrise-mark.svg` (through the locked 4096 master) stays the source of the Sunrise.
- iOS 18.4 to 25, which the app still supports, show flat icons generated from the same files, for the standard icon and every alternate.

## Non-goals

- No change to the macOS, Windows or Linux icons, the in-app marks or the website.
- Dawn is not added to the Mac.
- No Patron gating. As on desktop, every icon is free.
- No TestFlight upload, App Store Connect change or App Store screenshots without the owner's explicit yes.

## Anti-goals

- Icons whose standard Light look is still a flat Sunrise on cream. That is the problem this solves.
- A picker whose thumbnails don't match what the Home Screen then shows.
- Icon artwork that can't be rebuilt from a script.

- The ids and labels of every row but Dawn's are the settings schema's (`settings-schema/schema.json` `appIcons`). Only `sunrise-dawn` is new, and it stays out of the schema because the Mac doesn't offer it (see Parity).
- **My call:** Sunrise Dark is included so the iPhone offers everything the Mac does, plus Dawn. It keeps a dark tile even on a light Home Screen. That's different from iOS's own Dark style, which darkens every icon.
- **My call:** Sunrise Amber uses the Mac's definition rather than the slightly warmer tile drawn for option C. The two looked nearly alike in the proofs, and this way one file set serves both platforms. Sunrise Gold keeps option B's iPhone artwork, because its reflection and three glass pieces were the point of the owner's ask. The Mac's Sunrise Gold is a flatter design under the same name.

## Artwork

All six packages are written by a new module, `scripts/ios-app-icons.js`, which `scripts/build-brand-assets.js` calls. Each package is emitted through the existing `emit()`, so `brand:check` reports any stale file.

### Layers drawn from the master (Dawn and Gold)

- **Placement.** The 4096 master is cut and placed exactly like the Mac's native layer: the `SUNRISE_CROP` region, resized to 845 px at 89 px on the 1024 canvas.
- **Three silhouettes.** Its alpha is split into three by region, measured on the native layer:
  - **Water:** everything below the horizon gap (y ≥ 655).
  - **Sun:** everything above it within 224 px of the disc's center (510, 654).
  - **Rays:** the rest.
- **Bolder strokes.** Each silhouette is grown slightly (a Gaussian blur, then a threshold): rays σ 3, sun σ 2, water σ 1.5. Apple's guidance warns against very thin strokes under glass. At these sizes the two lowest water lines keep a visible gap.
- **Flat paint.** Each silhouette is painted flat. Icon Composer adds the glass, highlights and shadows, as Apple's guidance asks.
  - **Dawn:**
    - **Rays:** white.
    - **Sun:** a vertical gradient from #FFE08A to #FF9440.
    - **Water:** white, warming toward the center to #FFC98A. The reflection lives in the glass lines themselves, not in a drawn glow.
  - **Gold:** cream (#FFF7E4 for the sun, #FFFAEE for rays and water), with the water warming to white at its center.

### Backgrounds

An Icon Composer fill takes at most a two-color linear gradient, so the sky is drawn as full-bleed tile images.

- **Dawn tiles.**
  - **Light (sky):** #7FAEDF at the top, through #C3C3E2 and #F7C9A6, to #FFD59A at the horizon.
  - **Light (sea):** below the horizon, #F2B48E to #9C9FC6 to #6E8CC0.
  - **Dark (night):** #0E1530 to #2C2347 to #7A3E3A at the horizon, then #5A3238 to #0E1328 below it.
  - **Clear and Tinted:** both tiles are hidden, so iOS draws its own glass backdrop.
  - A soft light behind the sun is drawn in Light only.
- **Gold tiles.**
  - **Light:** a gold gradient (#FBE2A6, #EDBE6A, #C98B3A), with a soft light behind the sun and a reflection glow under the water.
  - **Dark:** a dark fill (#2A2218 to #14110D), with the glow at reduced opacity. The mark turns gold (#F0C46E sun, #E9B860 rays and water) through per-appearance layer fills.

  These glows are the one place the artwork draws light itself. They were part of option B as approved.

### Icon Composer groups, front to back

| Icon | Groups |
|---|---|
| Dawn | Water (glass, translucency 0.35, blur 0.6) · Sun (glass, layer-color shadow) · Rays (glass) · Tiles (sky, night and glow, not glass) |
| Gold | Water · Sun · Rays (each glass, layer-color shadow, translucency 0.35–0.45) · Tiles |

That is four groups, Apple's maximum. The exact values from the proof packages are listed in the appendix. The module records them in one place.

### Reused packages

- **Sunrise and Sunrise Dark** are written from `src/main/app-icon-assets.js` through `createIconDocument` (`scripts/after-pack-app-icons.js`), as the Mac builds them. The one difference is `supported-platforms`, which becomes `{ "circles": ["watchOS"], "squares": "shared" }`. Their layer is the existing `build/app-icons/Icon.icon/Assets/sunrise-mark.png`.
- **Sunrise Spectrum and Sunrise Amber** are built the same way from their Mac definitions and their layers in `build/app-icons/colorways/`.
- The PNGs are copied into each package, since Xcode compiles a package from its own `Assets` folder. `brand:check` confirms each copy matches its source byte for byte.

## Where the files go and how Xcode builds them

- The packages go in `ios/Blanc/Blanc/AppIcons/`: `AppIcon.icon` (Sunrise Gold), `SunriseDawn.icon`, `Sunrise.icon`, `SunriseDark.icon`, `SunriseSpectrum.icon` and `SunriseAmber.icon`. The target's folder is a file-system-synchronized group, so Xcode picks them up without project-file entries.
- `AppIcon.appiconset` is deleted. Apple's documentation says a `.icon` replaces an icon asset catalog, and Xcode generates the flat images older iOS versions need at build time.
  - **Checked:** `actool` with `--minimum-deployment-target 18.4` wrote `AppIcon60x60@2x.png` and `AppIcon76x76@2x~ipad.png` next to `Assets.car` for the primary `.icon`.
  - **Checked, alternates:** loose PNGs are written for the primary icon only. But `assetutil --info` shows that `Assets.car` holds flat 1024 px `Icon Image` renditions, with a `MultiSized Image` set, for every alternate as well as the primary, in the Any, Dark and Tintable appearances, for phone and pad. Asset-catalog alternate icons are looked up through `CFBundleIconName` in `Assets.car`, so iOS 18 should find them. The picker is therefore offered on every supported iOS version.
  - **Not checked:** iOS 18 itself. No iOS 18 simulator runtime is installed. The plan repeats the `assetutil` check on the real app build and says so.
- **Build settings, both configurations:**
  - `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_APPICON_NAME = AppIcon`, unchanged.
  - `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_ALTERNATE_APPICON_NAMES = "SunriseDawn Sunrise SunriseDark SunriseSpectrum SunriseAmber"`.
- **Checked:** with `actool`, `--alternate-app-icon` for `.icon` files writes `CFBundleAlternateIcons` entries.
- **Checked:** a test app on the iOS 27.1 simulator switched to the Gold glass icon with `setAlternateIconName`, and iOS showed its standard "You have changed the icon" notice (`switch-test.png`). The call fails with "operation was cancelled" if it is made before the app is active, so the picker only calls it from a tap.
- **Two Xcodes.** Local builds use Xcode 27.1. CI (`.github/workflows/ios.yml`) builds with the newest Xcode 26 on the `macos-26` image, and that run must pass. The packages use only keys Icon Composer 26 writes: `fill`, `linear-gradient`, `glass`, `shadow`, `translucency`, `specular`, `blur-material`, `lighting`, the `fill`/`hidden`/`opacity` specializations, and `"squares": "shared"`. If Xcode 26 still rejects something, the plan fixes the package shape before the PR merges.

## The picker

- **Where.** Settings → Appearance gets an **App icon** row under Theme, showing the current icon's name. It opens a list of six rows, each with a 60 pt preview, the label and a checkmark on the current icon.
- **Choosing.** A tap calls `UIApplication.setAlternateIconName`: `nil` for Sunrise Gold, otherwise the `.icon` name. iOS shows its own confirmation.
  - The checkmark follows `UIApplication.alternateIconName`, read again whenever the view appears and after each change.
  - An unknown name shows Sunrise Gold checked.
  - On failure, nothing changes.
- **No settings key.** iOS stores the choice itself. The setting is device-local on every platform and never synced. The `appIcon` value sent to internal pages is unchanged, and iOS still doesn't list `appIcon` among its page capabilities.
- **Copy.** The row title and the list's footer reuse `settings.general.appIconSetting.label` ("App icon") and `settings.general.appIconSetting.hint` ("Choose the Blanc icon shown by your system"), so no new strings are added. The icon names are product names and stay untranslated, as on desktop.
- **Code.**
  - `AppIconChoice.swift` holds the enum (order, `.icon` name, label). Shared labels come from the generated `BlancAppIcon`, and Dawn's label is local.
  - `AppIconSettingsView.swift` holds the list.
- **Accessibility.** Each row is a button labeled with the icon's name and has the selected trait when current. Identifiers are `settings.appIcon` for the row and `appIcon.<id>` for each choice.

### Previews

The picker must look like the Home Screen, so its previews are Icon Composer renders, not flat approximations.

- **Script.** `npm run ios:icon-previews` (macOS with Icon Composer only) renders each package with `ictool` at 180 px for the iOS platform, in the Default and Dark renditions. It writes them as `AppIconPreview-<Name>.imageset` in `Assets.xcassets`, with a dark-appearance variant, so SwiftUI picks the right one.
- **Lock.** It also writes `ios/Blanc/app-icon-previews.lock.json`, which records the sha256 of every input file of each package and of each preview. `ictool` isn't available in CI, so nothing re-renders there.
- **Stale previews.** A unit test fails when a package changes without its previews being re-rendered. The same pattern already protects the Sunrise master (`assets/sunrise-mark.lock.json`).

## Appendix: values from the proof packages

These are the values in the Dawn and Gold packages behind `first-options.png`. Colors are sRGB hex. Icon Composer stores them as `extended-srgb` channel fractions. Geometry is on the 1024 canvas.

### Silhouettes (both icons)

- **Split:** water is y ≥ 655. Sun is above that, within 224 px of (510, 654). Rays are the rest. Alpha below 4 is dropped first.
- **Grow:** a Gaussian blur of the alpha with σ 3 (rays), 2 (sun) or 1.5 (water). The blurred alpha then maps 0–40 → 0, 70–255 → 255, and linear in between.
- **Water warmth:** a radial gradient centered at (510, 660), radius 230, stretched 2.2× vertically. It holds the center color to 55% of the radius, then fades to the edge color.

### Dawn (`SunriseDawn.icon`)

- **Fill:** linear, top to bottom, #A9C6E6 → #F6CDA8. Dark: #121A33 → #3B2433. It only shows where no tile covers it.
- **Layer paint:**
  - Sun: a vertical gradient #FFE08A (y 440) → #FF9440 (y 654).
  - Rays: #FFFFFF.
  - Water: #FFC98A at the center, #FFFFFF at the edges.
- **Groups, front to back:**
  1. Water: glass, shadow neutral 0.3, translucency 0.35, specular, blur-material 0.6, lighting individual.
  2. Sun: glass, shadow layer-color 0.6, translucency 0.2, specular.
  3. Rays: glass, shadow neutral 0.3, translucency 0.35, specular.
  4. Tiles, not glass, back to front:
     - Sky: hidden in Dark and Tinted.
     - Night: shown only in Dark.
     - Glow: hidden in Dark and Tinted.
- **Sky tile:**
  - Down to y 654: #7FAEDF (0), #C3C3E2 (0.45), #F7C9A6 (0.8), #FFD59A (1).
  - From y 654 down: #F2B48E (0), #9C9FC6 (0.5), #6E8CC0 (1).
- **Night tile:**
  - Down to y 654: #0E1530 (0), #2C2347 (0.6), #7A3E3A (1).
  - From y 654 down: #5A3238 (0) → #0E1328 (1).
- **Glow:** a radial gradient centered at (510, 614), radius 330. #FFF3D8 at alpha 0.85 to 0.45 of the radius at alpha 0.47, then #FFE7C0 at alpha 0 at the edge.
- **In Dark,** the sun, rays and water keep their Light colors.

### Gold (`AppIcon.icon`)

- **Fill:** linear #F3CD82 → #C98B3A. Dark: #2A2218 → #14110D.
- **Layer paint:**
  - Sun: #FFF7E4.
  - Rays: #FFFAEE.
  - Water: #FFFFFF at the center, #FBE6BC at the edges.
- **Dark layer fills:** sun #F0C46E, rays and water #E9B860.
- **Groups, front to back:**
  1. Water: glass, shadow layer-color 0.55, translucency 0.45, specular, lighting individual.
  2. Sun: as Water, translucency 0.35.
  3. Rays: as Sun.
  4. Tiles, not glass, back to front:
     - Tile: hidden in Dark and Tinted.
     - Glow: hidden in Tinted, opacity 0.35 in Dark.
     - Reflection: hidden in Tinted.
- **Tile:** vertical #FBE2A6 (0), #EDBE6A (0.45), #C98B3A (1).
- **Glow:** as Dawn's, with #FFF1CC → #FFE2A0, radius 300, alpha 0.55.
- **Reflection:** an ellipse centered at (510, 774), radii 230 × 210. Its radial gradient is centered at (510, 684), radius 260, squeezed to 0.62 horizontally about (510, 654). It goes #FFF1CF from alpha 0.9, through alpha 0.54 at 0.55 of the radius, to alpha 0 at the edge.
