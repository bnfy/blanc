## Release contents

- Mahjong adds Pyramid, Fortress, Butterfly, Bridge, and Cross beside Turtle,
  Arch, and Peaks. Deals remain solvable by construction, and Daily rotates
  deterministically across all eight layouts.
- A device-local Records sheet shows total boards cleared, current and longest
  daily streaks, dailies cleared, per-board Classic and Burst bests, and the
  last four weeks of Daily results.
- New Mahjong tabs remember the most recent layout, mode, and deal choice and
  can offer to continue an unfinished board already open in another tab.
- Shuffle is undoable, including from the Burst rescue flow. Tile-face numerals
  and wind badges retain the full ExtraBold JetBrains Mono face while the game
  UI remains in Inter.
- Notices use lacquer pills inside the table; completion totals are counted
  once even if persistence maintenance fails; and the release includes the
  documented hint, Daily-card, dead-end, first-clear, and result-display fixes.
