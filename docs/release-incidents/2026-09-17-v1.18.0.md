Status: public and deployed. Published at `2026-09-18T03:36:53Z` from immutable source
`0a42e5d0e77648852e5c8a6d239b24a4decc4ea3`. Native publication, independent

Release: <https://github.com/bnfy/blanc/releases/tag/v1.18.0>.

## Scope

- Sync has an explicit first-device and join-another-device setup. The join
  path checks the supplied name and passphrase before credentials are saved,
  preventing a typo from silently creating a second empty sync account.
- A Personal-only start-page migration checklist combines Sync and Bring Your
  Tabs, retains device-local completion, can be hidden, and retires at `2/2`.
  `/sync` opens the Sync section directly, and Sync is second in Settings.
- Sync settings retain the prior operational controls: sync now, turn off,
  optional remote-data deletion, open-tab sharing, status, last-sync time, and
  the single last-error row.
- Electron advanced to 44.4.2, carrying Chromium 152.0.7977.130. The macOS
  bundle build number advanced to 1180.
