## Scope

The release prevents pre-restore Profile Sync activity from retracting live
synced tabs and favicons, while preserving immediate retractions when tab
sharing is explicitly turned off. It also broadens favicon compatibility for
large app artwork and generically served PNGs, adds domain-initial fallbacks,
and keeps the surface around permission prompts transparent.
