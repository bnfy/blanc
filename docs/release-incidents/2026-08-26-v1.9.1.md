## Scope

The release prevents pre-restore Profile Sync activity from retracting live
synced tabs and favicons, while preserving immediate retractions when tab
sharing is explicitly turned off. It also broadens favicon compatibility for
large app artwork and generically served PNGs, adds domain-initial fallbacks,
and keeps the surface around permission prompts transparent.

## Follow-up evidence

Publication does not itself prove an updater restart handoff. The real public
v1.9.0 → v1.9.1 **Restart Now** flows on macOS and Windows remain to be run and
recorded, and the downloaded public v1.9.1 AppImage still needs a launch/render
confirmation. Until those checks are added here, do not claim them from a
direct installer launch, the local migration smoke, or the CI build alone.
