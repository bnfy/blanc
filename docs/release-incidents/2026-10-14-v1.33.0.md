# Blanc v1.33.0 release — October 14, 2026

Status: published and independently verified; public v1.33.0 is the baseline.
The owner confirmed both adjacent public-feed updater handoffs (v1.32.0 →
v1.33.0), on macOS and on Windows.

Scope: a minor release with these app changes merged since v1.32.0.

- Blanc Spaces is free for everyone (#785). Blanc Patron is optional support
  and unlocks nothing: anyone can create, save and import spaces, and the Arc
  import always creates spaces (the one-window-per-Space path is gone).
- The Blanc Spaces redesign (#786, #787, #788). Each space has a colour and a
  symbol, shown as the space mark at the start of the Island; a click opens
  the rebuilt Spaces menu (edit in place, delete with Undo, drag or
  Option/Alt+↑/↓ to reorder). The Spaces glyph is a ringed planet (D20), a
  space's colour is drawn exactly with a white or black symbol (D21), and
  Recently Deleted rows show the space's tile and when it was deleted.
- Switching spaces is never refused for a limit (#789, D22). The "Your
  retained spaces are full" refusal is gone; afterwards Blanc releases set-aside
  spaces beyond six and, unless Quiet Tabs is off, quiets the oldest set-aside
  spaces' eligible tabs.
- On Linux, Quiet Tabs now quiets a page that keeps session storage by ending
  its verified renderer (#789, `terminateRenderer`). Electron's
  `forcefullyCrashRenderer()` only asks a Linux renderer to crash itself, and
  under the user-namespace sandbox it never did, so such tabs stayed loaded.
  #789 also added a `quiet-tabs` CI job on Linux and Windows.
- Interface localization (F44, #781): a machine-translated Italian interface,
  and the `/theme` hint and reference in Spanish, French and Portuguese now
  show the words the command accepts.
- 1Password fill on Windows and Linux stays enabled and **unannounced**, under
  the 2026-10-12 waiver in [the 1Password integration doc](../1password-integration.md).
  Nothing about it changed in this release.
- #790 is test-only.

Release notes: [v1.33.0](../press/release-notes/v1.33.0.md). Stable mode,
platforms `mac,windows,linux`, `BLANC_MAC_ARCHES=arm64,x64`, from
`3e761786666174bab6600b645f9cb34491f5f247` (PR #793). Electron stays at
44.5.1, the runtime the uBO platform acceptance covers; 44.7.2 was available.

Terminal.app window, and passed every gate on the first attempt. Safari already

`grep --line-buffered`, no `cut`, `tr` or `head`).

## Updater handoffs

Each counts only when it starts inside public v1.32.0, which then finds,
downloads and installs v1.33.0 through **Restart Now**. Opening an installer
directly does not count.

On October 14, 2026 the owner confirmed the macOS update from public v1.32.0
to v1.33.0 inside Blanc, clicking **Restart Now**. The installed app's
updater log shows public v1.32.0 finding 1.33.0 at `05:30:54Z`, downloading
`Blanc-1.33.0-arm64-mac.zip` (a full 160.5 MB download), and Squirrel.Mac
staging it at `05:31:01Z`. At `05:31:08Z` the relaunched app checked again and
reported 1.33.0 as current. Afterwards the installed `/Applications/Blanc.app`
reports 1.33.0 (1330), arm64, passes strict deep `codesign` verification, and
Gatekeeper accepts it as a notarized Developer ID app.

On October 14, 2026 the owner also confirmed the Windows update, in the
Windows 11 VM's normal per-user install, clicking **Restart Now**. That
install's `updater.log` shows the handoff from inside public v1.32.0: it found
1.33.0 at `12:35:48Z`, downloaded `Blanc-Setup-1.33.0.exe` by `12:36:15Z`,
installed on explicit `quitAndInstall`, and ran that installer with
`--updated /S --force-run`. At `12:37:39Z` the relaunched app checked again
and reported 1.33.0 as current, so Blanc reopened by itself. The
`quitAndInstall` and installer lines carry a `2026-10-13T12:39:49Z` timestamp,
a day behind the lines around them; the VM's clock was still catching up
after boot, and the lines name the 1.33.0 installer and sit between the
download and the relaunched check. The installed `Blanc.exe` reports 1.33.0
with a valid, timestamped Authenticode signature for `CN=Bananify Creative`.
A separate manual close-and-relaunch check was not reported.
