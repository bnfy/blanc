## Found during the release: the Shared Spaces index hint (#1314)

While attempt 3 was in its gate, the owner reported that a tab moved into a group on the Mac showed ungrouped on the iPhone. The phone's own data, read over USB, showed:
- Its copy of the Space was from 5:52 pm New York time; the Mac's was from 6:54 pm.
- At 6:39 pm the phone had published a 47-minute-old index receipt over the Mac's newer revision hint. A receipt with a different revision always outranked the current hint.
- The phone's blob matched the regressed hint, so it never fetched again.

1.46's own change (#1298) makes desktops fetch only Spaces whose hint moved, so shipping without a fix would have spread the stall to desktops.

#1314 fixes it on both platforms:
- A receipt names the hint it was built over and is dropped once another device has published over it.
- A download the hint doesn't describe publishes a correction.
- A sync with nothing to write keeps its refreshed receipt.

Its tests reproduce the phone's sequence. The iPhone's half ships in the iPhone app's next build.

The owner had deleted the affected Spaces on the Mac at 6:54 pm. The phone kept Default as a local copy with a dormant link, as designed for a Space that's shown when another device deletes it.
