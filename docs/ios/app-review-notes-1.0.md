1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Blanc is a free web browser for iPhone from Bananify Creative, the companion to Blanc for Mac, Windows and Linux. It is for people who want a calm, private browser: ads and trackers are blocked by default, no account is needed, and the interface is one bar at the bottom of the screen (the Island) instead of a toolbar and tab strip. People who also use Blanc on a computer can see that computer's tabs and share Spaces (saved sets of tabs) with their phone.

2. SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES
No sign-in, demo account or sample files are needed. Then:
- Browse: tap the address in the Island at the bottom, type an address or a search, and press Return. + opens a new tab.
- The Sunrise button in the Island opens a menu: New Private Tab, Find in Page, Share, Send to Device, Save to Favorites, and Ad & tracker blocking for the current site.
- The tab count in the Island shows all tabs.
- Tapping the address also lists open tabs and has buttons for Favorites, History and Settings.
- Settings: search engine, theme, app icon (Appearance → App icon), Start Page, Privacy (Blanc Blocker, allowed sites, Clear cookies, cache & site data, and the anonymous usage count switch) and Sync.
- Share extension: in Safari, tap Share and choose Blanc to open the page there.
- Optional Sync: Blanc for desktop creates the Sync; the iPhone joins it in Settings → Sync by scanning a QR code or typing a pairing code shown on the computer. The iPhone app has no sign-up, and Settings → Sync → Disconnect removes the phone. Every other feature works without Sync. Blanc for desktop is free at https://blanc.day/download.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- WebKit renders all web pages.
- The search engine the user picks: DuckDuckGo (default), Google, Bing, Brave Search or Kagi. Searches go straight to that engine.
- Blanc Sync (optional): a Bananify Creative service on Cloudflare Workers that stores end-to-end encrypted data (tabs, Favorites, settings, Spaces). The service cannot read it.
- Usage count (optional, can be turned off in Settings → Privacy): one anonymous count per launch sent to a Bananify Creative service on Cloudflare Workers.
- Ad blocking uses the EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists, bundled in the app. No lists are downloaded at runtime.
No authentication, payment, data-provider or AI services are used. There are no in-app purchases, no sign-up in the app and no user-generated content.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in every region where it is offered. It is not offered in China mainland.
