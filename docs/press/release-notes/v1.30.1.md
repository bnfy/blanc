# Blanc 1.30.1

## Fixed

- The Island, Blanc's floating address and tab bar, grows slightly as your
  pointer approaches it. That movement is now smooth at any display refresh
  rate. It used to update about 60 times a second, which looked stop-start on
  120 Hz and faster screens.
- Activating a Blanc Patron license key while Polar, the payment service, is
  busy or down now says Polar is not responding and to try again in a few
  minutes, instead of saying the key could not be activated.
- When Blanc checks an active Patron license and Polar reports that the key is
  missing, revoked, or expired, Patron now ends. Before, that answer was
  treated like an outage. A Polar outage still keeps Patron active for up to
  30 days, as before.
