# Blanc 1.31.0

## Changed

- The start page footer is reorganized. The "ready to move in?" checklist is
  now a **Finish setup** pill on the left of the footer that opens the list;
  its × hides the checklist for good, as before. **Customize** sits alone in
  the middle.
- The Blanc Patron reminder is now one **Upgrade to Blanc Patron** pill in the
  start page footer. Its × hides it on this device for 90 days. Patrons and
  private tabs still never see it.
- Billboard's clock, recent sites and groups are centered vertically in the
  window, and the clock no longer shows AM/PM.
- Start page, onboarding and Settings headings use a narrower cut of Blanc's
  serif typeface, the same one as blancbrowser.com, and the Billboard clock is
  set in a lighter weight.
- Private tabs are marked more consistently: the "private" chip in the Island,
  the tag in the expanded tab list and the marker in vertical tabs share one
  dashed style, and the tag lines up on every row.
- Blanc 1.31.0 and later report two facts on their first day, if usage
  measurement is on: whether Blanc is your default browser, and whether you
  opened three web pages in regular tabs. Nothing about the pages is sent.
  The privacy page and FAQ explain the details.

## Fixed

- Opening the Island by clicking it no longer makes it blink, and a slow frame
  now pauses the expand instead of making it jump ahead.
- The Tabs step of Bring Your Tabs shows every tab in each window. A window
  used to show only two or three, with no way to scroll to the rest.
