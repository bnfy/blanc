# Blanc 1.42.1

## Changed

- The floating Glance card stands out more from the page behind it: it casts
  a deeper shadow, its corners are rounder, and the page inside it sits
  slightly inset under its header.

## Fixed

- On Windows, with Auto-hide set to Float, the floating Island no longer
  loses its rounded ends when it gets wider, for example after you open more
  tabs or visit a site with a longer address.
