# Blanc 1.20.0

## Improved

- **Mahjong now opens as its own tab.** Launch it from the footer of any start-page layout. Your current start page stays open, and an unfinished game is still available through Mahjong's Continue prompt. Existing direct Mahjong links keep working.
- Start pages now offer four layouts: Ledger, Billboard, Shelf, and Tally. If Mahjong was your selected layout, Blanc moves that preference to Billboard without changing other layout choices or saved games.
- Mahjong keeps its familiar green lacquer table and tile artwork, with a Sunrise mark in its header and quieter framing. Private Mahjong retains its separate purple treatment and prominent private indicator.
- Links that return a browser sign-in to an installed app can now discover its registered handler and ask for your permission, including callbacks beyond the previously reviewed app schemes. Browser-internal and dangerous system schemes remain blocked. Repeated page prompts wait for another click or activation key.
