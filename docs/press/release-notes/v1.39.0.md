# Blanc 1.39.0

## Added

- **Auto-hide the Island.** Turn on **Auto-hide the Island** in Settings →
  General, from the View menu, or with ⌘⌥B on macOS (Ctrl+Alt+B on Windows
  and Linux), and the page fills the whole window. Point at the top edge of
  the window, rest the pointer near the top, or press ⌘L (Ctrl+L) to bring
  the Island back; it slides away again once you move off it. It's off by
  default.
- **Tab Archive.** Turn on **Archive inactive tabs** in Settings → General,
  and loose tabs you haven't looked at for 12 hours, a day, 7 days or 30 days
  close themselves into an Archive. Pinned, grouped and private tabs are
  never archived, and neither is a tab that is playing media or holding
  something you typed. Find archived tabs in the ⌘L panel, or in History →
  **Archived tabs** (or `/archive`), and restore them with one click. The
  Archive stays on this device and keeps each tab for 30 days. It's off by
  default; a new profile offers it during setup.
- **Sunrise Gold.** A new macOS app icon: the Sunrise in glass over gold,
  soft in Light and polished in Dark. Choose it in Settings → General → App
  icon.
- **What's New.** After an update that adds features, Blanc opens a page on
  blanc.day showing the highlights, once.

## Changed

- **Settings → General is grouped into cards:** Island, Appearance, Start
  Page, Search and Browsing. The theme setting is now labeled **Theme**.
- **A panel opened by hover closes when you move away** from it, unless you
  used it.
- **On macOS, the window buttons sit where Safari and Finder put them.**
