# Blanc 1.43.0

## Added

- **Protected video playback.** Blanc now plays video and music that sites
  protect with Widevine DRM (copy protection), on macOS (Apple Silicon and
  Intel), Windows and Linux. The first time Blanc starts after the update, it
  downloads the Widevine component in the background, and you can keep
  browsing meanwhile. **Protected media** in Settings → Help shows when the
  component is ready. On Linux, Blanc asks you to **Restart Blanc** once the
  component is installed, and again after the component updates; on any
  system the same button appears if setup fails or takes too long. Netflix and
  Spotify were tried on an Apple Silicon Mac. Whether a particular site plays
  still depends on that site.
- **Sunrise Spectrum and Sunrise Amber.** Two new macOS app icons: the Sunrise
  redrawn in glass as a ring sun, rays and water lines inside a white outline.
  Spectrum is coral and red over royal blue and navy on a gray tile; Amber is
  gold over bronze on a cream tile. Both tiles turn dark in Dark mode. Choose
  one in Settings → General → App icon.

## Changed

- **Pinned tabs can join a Split View.** A split is now pinned or unpinned as
  a whole. Split a pinned tab with any other tab, or press **Keep as split**
  on a Glance card over a pinned page, and the split is pinned. **Pin Split**
  and **Unpin Split** in either tab's menu pin or unpin both tabs. A pinned
  split is one row under Pinned in the ⌘L panel and in vertical tabs, stays
  pinned after a relaunch, and comes back pinned with Reopen Closed Tab.
  Before, pinned tabs couldn't join a split, and pinning a tab took it out of
  its split.
- Blanc now runs on castLabs' Electron for Content Security 44.9.3, a build of
  Electron that can play Widevine-protected media, instead of Electron 44.8.0.
  Chromium stays at 152.0.7977.167. The optional uBlock Origin blocker remains
  available on this version.
- On macOS, an HTML file you open from Finder or another app now opens in its
  own window as a static preview instead of in a Blanc tab. Its scripts don't
  run, and it can't load anything from the network or from other files, so
  only what is inside the file itself shows.

## Fixed

- A page you opened while Blanc was still starting could show a "blocked"
  error page even though the page itself had loaded. Blanc holds web pages for
  a moment at startup until its blocker is ready, then opens them; the error
  no longer appears over a page that opened normally.
