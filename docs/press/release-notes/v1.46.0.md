# Blanc 1.46.0

## Added

- **Island size.** Settings → General → **Island size** makes the Island and
  its buttons bigger: choose Default, Large or Extra large. The site address
  grows with it, and the page moves down to make room. It's set on each
  device.
- **Mini Window.** A small Blanc window for looking something up or opening a
  link without leaving the app you're in. Open one from File → **New Mini
  Window**, from Blanc's Dock menu on a Mac, or with a shortcut that works in
  any app while Blanc is running: turn it on in Settings → General → **Mini
  window shortcut** (⌥⌘N or ⌃⌥Spacebar on a Mac, Ctrl+Alt+Spacebar or
  Ctrl+Shift+Spacebar on Windows and Linux). The shortcut is off by default,
  and Settings says so if it can't be used. A mini window isn't
  saved, links from other apps never open in it, and it closes after an hour
  unused unless a page in it is in use or has unsaved changes; closing its
  last tab closes it too. Choose **Open in Blanc** to keep it as an ordinary
  window.
- **Ctrl+Tab by most recent use on the Mac.** Ctrl+Tab now goes back through
  the tabs you used last on macOS too, as it already does on Windows and
  Linux: hold Ctrl and press Tab, then let go to switch, or press Esc to stay
  where you are. To go by position instead, choose **In the order of the tab
  list** in Settings → General → **Switch tabs with Ctrl+Tab**.

## Changed

- **Sync checks for changes more often.** While a Blanc window is in front,
  Blanc checks for changes from your other devices every 15 seconds instead of
  once a minute, and downloads only what changed.
- **A cleaner Glance card.** The Glance card no longer draws a border or a line
  around its page; its shadow sets it apart, and its GLANCE label brightens
  when it has focus.

## Fixed

- With Spaces sync on, the **Choose Spaces** sheet opens at once instead of
  waiting behind "Sync is busy; retrying.", **Turn on** closes it as soon as
  your choice is saved while the upload carries on in the background, and
  **Cancel** always works.
- A Space you save while Spaces sync is on shares itself and reaches your other
  devices in seconds, instead of waiting a minute or more, including a Space
  whose only tab is the New Tab page.
